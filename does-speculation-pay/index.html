Model: baseline D + n·t; parallel dispatch divides tool time by width; streaming overlap hides min(h·E, D) under decode. Backend saturation raises effective latency in proportion to in-flight calls over capacity, and wasted speculation adds to that in-flight count without returning anything, which is what drives the break-even point in chart C. A deliberately crude queueing term: enough to show where the wall is, not enough to size a cluster. Arithmetic only; no measurements of any specific deployment.
